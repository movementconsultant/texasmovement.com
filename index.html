ABOUT TMI

Why Texas Movement exists.

Texas Movement International is a multi-vertical systems company headquartered in Texas. We build the infrastructure — athletic, economic, cultural, and operational — that helps athletes, founders, cities, and families move with precision.

Our verticals span performance science, media production, footwear, consulting, and ceremony technology.

TMI exists because high-functioning people and institutions need systems that work together, not just individual services. Every TMI product is designed to integrate with the broader ecosystem: better athletes generate better stories; better stories create better markets; better markets fund better infrastructure. We build for compounding returns.

Systems, not one-offs.

The through-line is simple: put high-functioning people and places under good load. When the training system, the media story, the product, and the capital all point in the same direction, the work compounds instead of leaking.

Step into the network.

As TMI grows across media, performance, and city-scale work, we’re selectively opening the door to future hosts and on-air talent who want to carry specific shows, verticals, or markets forward. This isn’t a collab request — it’s a long-term creative and operational role inside a system that’s being built to last.

  • You’re comfortable on camera and have documented reps — interviews, commentary, training content, or event coverage you can point to.
  • You understand at least one of the following at a real level: elite sports and performance, founder and operator economics, city development and infrastructure, or ceremony and community design.
  • You think in systems, not moments — you’re interested in building a body of work, not chasing individual clips.
  • You can represent the TMI brand without supervision: calm, credible, economics-first, and serious about the work.
  • You’re based in or connected to Texas, the South, or a city market TMI operates in — or you have a clear case for why you should be.

To apply: send a short introduction, links to work you’ve already produced, and a note on what show or vertical you’d want to host or build to texasmovementmedia@gmail.com with the subject line: Host Application: [Your Name / Market].
