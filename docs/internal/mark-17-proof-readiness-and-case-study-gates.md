# Mark 17 (internal) — Proof Readiness and Case-Study Gates

Internal-only. Not rendered, not imported, not linked from any public route.
A private system that permits future proof to be built honestly, without
fabricating any of it now. Mirrors the existing proof-registry pattern in
`docs/mark-5-tmi-content-model-and-proof-registry.md`, extended for the
Digital Ecosystem Audit flagship specifically.

**Boundary rule** (applies throughout):

> Current industry guidance can inform TMI's private operating baseline. It
> does not establish that TMI is certified, compliant, secure, safe,
> effective, qualified, operational, ready for public use, or authorized to
> make public claims.

**No customer/client claim, testimonial, result, logo, name, metric, or
story may be invented or displayed before actual evidence and permission
exist.** Every field below is a template, not a populated record.

## Proof-candidate template

Copy this template once per future proof candidate:

- **Proof candidate:** _(blank)_
- **Source artifact:** _(blank — the actual document/email/deliverable
  that would substantiate this)_
- **Factual claim:** _(blank — stated as narrowly and specifically as the
  evidence supports, never rounded up)_
- **Date/time period:** _(blank)_
- **Permissions:** _(blank — explicit, recorded, client-granted permission
  to reference this at all, separate from permission to name the client)_
- **Confidentiality level:** one of — `fully confidential (internal only)`
  / `client-approved for anonymized reference` / `client-approved for named
  reference`
- **Client/public naming status:** one of — `not named` / `named, pending
  written approval` / `named, approved`
- **Evidence quality:** one of — `anecdotal` / `documented internally` /
  `documented and client-confirmed` / `independently verifiable`
- **Required review:** _(blank — who internally must review this before it
  moves status, e.g. the client-facing account owner plus a second
  reviewer)_
- **Allowed public wording:** _(blank — drafted only after all of the
  above are populated and reviewed; defaults to none)_
- **Prohibited wording:** any absolute/superiority language ("best,"
  "leading," "guaranteed," "proven") regardless of how strong the
  underlying evidence is — this list applies to every proof candidate
  without exception.
- **Metric evidence:** _(blank — the literal underlying number/fact, with
  its source, never a rounded or estimated figure)_
- **Claim risk:** one of — `low` / `medium` / `high` / `restricted-domain

- **Case-study status:** one of — `absent` / `internal candidate` /

- **Expiration/revalidation:** _(blank — no proof is permanently valid;

- **Correction/removal protocol:** any proof candidate found to be
  inaccurate, outdated, or withdrawn by the client must be removed from

## First-three-engagement proof plan

A deliberate sequence, so evidence quality builds honestly over time
instead of being asserted from the first engagement:

### Engagement 1 — Internal process validation

- **Goal:** confirm the Audit→Architect→Build→Compound blueprint actually
  works end-to-end, before asking any client to be a reference.
- **Evidence produced:** internal-only process notes — what worked, what
  didn't, where the blueprint needs revision.
- **Public use:** none. Engagement 1 is explicitly not a case-study
  candidate.
- **Owner decision required:** whether the blueprint needs revision before
  a second engagement.

### Engagement 2 — Refined delivery and consent/permission capture

- **Goal:** deliver against the (possibly revised) blueprint, and this
  time, explicitly build the consent/permission-capture step into the
  engagement agreement from the start.
- **Evidence produced:** a completed engagement with client-granted
  permission on file for at least an anonymized future reference,
  contingent on delivery quality.
- **Public use:** none yet — permission captured, not exercised.
- **Owner decision required:** whether delivery quality met the bar for
  even an anonymized future reference.

### Engagement 3 — Candidate for a high-quality evidence-based case study

- **Goal:** the first engagement genuinely eligible to become a public
  case study, having benefited from two prior rounds of process
  refinement.
- **Evidence produced:** a documented, client-confirmed outcome with
  explicit naming permission (if granted) or anonymized-reference
  permission at minimum.
- **Public use:** still gated — reaching "case-study status: approved for
  future public-review" here does not itself authorize publication; that
  requires the same owner decision and public-claims-boundary review as
  everything else in this framework (see
  `docs/internal/mark-15-public-claims-boundary-guide.md`).
- **Owner decision required:** the actual publication decision, plus final
  wording review against the prohibited-wording list above.

---

## Why this sequence, not immediate proof

Treating Engagement 3 (not Engagement 1) as the earliest realistic
case-study candidate is a deliberate design choice: it prevents the
single most common integrity failure in early-stage consulting — rushing
an unrefined first engagement into a public case study before the
delivery process itself has been validated. No engagement has occurred
yet; this is a sequencing plan for when one does.
