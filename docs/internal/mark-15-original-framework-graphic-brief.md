# Mark 15 (internal) — Original Framework Graphic Brief

Internal-only. Not rendered, not imported, not linked from any public route.
**Text-only creative brief for a possible future graphic. No image is
generated by this task. No image asset, file, or reference is added to this
repository by this task.** Any actual image generation or design work
requires a separate, explicitly owner-approved task.

## Purpose

A single original conceptual diagram illuminating the TMI future-firm
vision as an operating system — how the pieces relate structurally, not a
literal org chart, product map, or marketing graphic. Intended for internal
planning/presentation use first; any public use would require a separate
owner decision after this brief's boundaries are respected in the final
execution.

## Structure to depict

- **Center:** TMI "Operating Core" — the umbrella/company layer itself.
- **Surrounding controlled lanes** (inner ring, structurally "active/
  building" tier): Consulting, Media, Performance, Distribution, Partners.
- **Outer governed/incubation ring** (structurally distinct, visually
  further from center or otherwise clearly demarcated as a different
  maturity tier): Health, FounderLink, Social/Gather, Reparations.
- **Separate connected founder layer:** AVM / Founder Systems Archive —
  drawn as its own node, connected to but visually distinct from the
  Operating Core (matching the established Mark 13/14 distinction: related
  structural grammar, different identity — see
  `docs/internal/mark-13-operating-console-vs-founder-archive-decision-record.md`).
- **Separate external commerce boundary:** HERO / external storefront,
  labeled internally unaudited — drawn with a visually distinct boundary
  treatment (e.g. a dashed or differently-weighted edge) signaling "outside
  this system, not internally verified," consistent with its existing
  site-wide "External storefront / internally unaudited" posture.
- **Underlay (background layer, not a literal flowchart):** a four-stage
  progression — Evidence → Governance → Operations → Public Surface —
  representing this framework's own adoption hierarchy (Tiers 1–4 in
  `docs/mark-15-external-standards-adoption-framework.md`), rendered as
  abstract structural bands or gradients beneath the node topology, not as
  a separate labeled flowchart competing with the main diagram.

## Explicit exclusions

The graphic must contain **none** of the following:

- Metrics, counters, statistics, or numbers of any kind.
- Live/status indicators, activity feeds, uptime, or "online" cues of any
  kind.
- Countdowns, dates, or timelines.
- Athlete imagery, patient imagery, medical imagery, or any depiction of a
  person in a clinical, therapeutic, or performance context.
- Political imagery or symbolism of any kind.
- LA28, Olympic, or Paralympic imagery, marks, or symbolism of any kind.
- Any third-party logo, trademark, brand mark, or external standards-body
  mark (e.g. no ISO/WCAG/regulatory-body seals or logos).
- Any claim, slogan, or promotional text baked into the graphic itself —
  labels should be structural/descriptive only (e.g. "Operating Core," not
  a tagline).
- Any resemblance to, or copy of, any specific external standards
  framework's published diagram, chart, or proprietary visual language —
  this must be an original topology, not an adaptation of a real standard's
  graphic.

## Visual direction

Consistent with the established TMI dark-first "Future-Firm Operating
Console" system (`docs/mark-13-tmi-future-firm-operating-console.md`):

- Near-black field as the base.
- Structural panel lines / hairline dividers separating the rings and
  nodes — not soft blends or gradients between structural regions.
- The restrained signal-green accent (`--ff-accent-signal`) used sparingly
  — e.g. to highlight a single relationship line or the Operating Core's
  boundary — never as a dominant fill across the whole graphic.
- Original, abstract topology: circles, rings, connecting lines, and
  labeled nodes — not literal building/office/product iconography, not
  isometric "SaaS illustration" style, not a stock-diagram template look.
- Systems-map clarity: a viewer unfamiliar with TMI should be able to read
  the tiering (core → controlled lanes → governed ring → connected founder
  layer → external boundary) at a glance, without needing the underlay
  explained.

- Not a request to generate the graphic now.
- Not an approval to publish the graphic once made — that is a separate,

  made consistent with the live token values in
  `texasmovement.com/src/styles/global.css` at the time of execution (not
  hardcoded here, since those values could evolve before this brief is
  acted on).
