# Mark 17 (internal) — Future-Firm Vision Graphic Brief

Internal-only. Not rendered, not imported, not linked from any public route.
**Text-only creative brief for a possible future graphic. No image is
generated by this task. No image asset, file, or reference is added to this
repository by this task.** Extends and updates the Mark 15 brief
(`docs/internal/mark-15-original-framework-graphic-brief.md`) to fold in
the Audit→Architect→Build→Compound methodology; any actual image
generation or design work requires a separate, explicitly owner-approved
task.

**Boundary rule** (applies throughout):

> Current industry guidance can inform TMI's private operating baseline. It
> does not establish that TMI is certified, compliant, secure, safe,
> effective, qualified, operational, ready for public use, or authorized to
> make public claims.

## Purpose

A single original conceptual diagram illustrating the TMI strategy as an
operating system — how the pieces relate structurally, not a literal org
chart, product map, sales funnel, or marketing graphic. Internal planning/
presentation use first; any public use requires a separate future owner
decision, same as every other artifact in this framework.

## Required composition

- **Center:** "TMI Operating Core"
- **Inner ring:** Audit → Architect → Build → Compound (the delivery
  methodology from `docs/internal/mark-17-digital-ecosystem-audit-delivery-blueprint.md`,
  rendered as a cyclical or sequential ring immediately around the core —
  not a separate competing diagram)
- **Primary lanes:** Consulting / Media / Performance / Distribution /
  Partners
- **Separate founder layer:** AVM Founder Systems Archive — its own node,
  connected to but visually distinct from the Operating Core, per the
  Mark 13/14 distinction
- **External commerce boundary:** HERO / separately operated commerce
  boundary — visually distinct edge treatment signaling "outside this
  system, not internally audited," consistent with its existing
  site-wide posture
- **Governed/restricted ring:** Health / FounderLink / Social-Gather /
  Reparations — a structurally distinct outer tier from the primary lanes,
  signaling lower maturity/higher governance requirement, not absence
- **Underlay:** Evidence → Governance → Operations → Public Surface —
  abstract structural bands beneath the node topology, representing the
  Mark 15 adoption-hierarchy tiers, not a separate competing flowchart

## Visual direction

Consistent with the established TMI dark-first "Future-Firm Operating
Console" system (`docs/mark-13-tmi-future-firm-operating-console.md`):

- Near-black field as the base.
- Structural linework — hairline dividers separating rings, lanes, and
  the inner methodology cycle — not soft blends or gradients between
  structural regions.
- Muted panels for each lane/node's background, consistent with the
  existing `--ff-bg-panel` token language.
- The restrained signal-green accent (`--ff-accent-signal`) used
  sparingly — e.g. a single relationship line, the inner
  Audit→Architect→Build→Compound ring's connecting arrows, or the
  Operating Core's boundary — never as a dominant fill.
- Original topology: circles, rings, connecting lines, and labeled nodes —
  not literal building/office/product iconography, not isometric "SaaS
  illustration" style, not a stock-diagram template look.
- Calm institutional hierarchy: a viewer unfamiliar with TMI should read
  the tiering (core → methodology cycle → primary lanes → founder layer →
  external boundary → governed ring) at a glance, without the underlay
  needing separate explanation.

## Explicit exclusions

The graphic must contain **none** of the following:

- Metrics, counters, or numbers of any kind.
- Live/status indicators, activity feeds, or fake data of any kind.
- Charts, graphs, or dashboards of any kind.
- Countdowns or dates.
- Public customer/client claims of any kind (no logos, no names, no
  testimonial-shaped elements).
- Athlete imagery, medical imagery, legal imagery, or political imagery of
  any kind.
- LA28, Olympic, or Paralympic imagery, marks, or symbolism of any kind.
- Any third-party logo, trademark, brand mark, platform logo, or external
  standards-body mark.
- Any claim, slogan, or promotional text baked into the graphic — labels
  should be structural/descriptive only.
- Any resemblance to, or copy of, any specific external framework's
  published diagram — this must be an original topology.
