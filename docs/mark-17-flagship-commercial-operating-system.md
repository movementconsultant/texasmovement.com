## Strategic framing

TMI is not attempting to out-compete legal firms, IT shops, trainers,
apparel brands, footwear companies, media companies, or regulated
professionals on their own terms. The hypothesized edge is **integrated
infrastructure plus cultural intelligence** — a platform advantage, not an
automatic claim of market leadership, superiority, compliance, safety,
professional qualification, performance, financial success, client
results, or operational maturity. Nothing in this document or its
companions asserts that edge as proven; it is a working hypothesis this
framework exists to test carefully, later, with real evidence.

The immediate objective is not vertical expansion — it is defining **one
narrow, repeatable flagship consulting engagement** (working label:
**Digital Ecosystem Audit**) that could later generate evidence through
high-quality delivery, following an **Audit → Architect → Build →
Compound** methodology shape.

## Part C — Ideal buyer and qualification model

**TMI does not currently serve, represent, or have results for any buyer
category below.** This is a hypothesis for future qualification, not a
statement of current activity.

**Allowed target categories:** founder-led organizations; independent
creators; public-facing talent; athletes/artists/journalists/entertainers/
speakers where a clearly bounded systems/brand/media need exists;
culture-facing organizations building owned digital infrastructure.

- **Buyer profile hypothesis:** an individual or small organization with
  real cultural or professional standing, fragmented across too many
  disconnected tools/platforms/vendors, who wants owned infrastructure
  instead of continued platform dependency.
- **Likely trigger:** a visible failure or friction point — a broken site,
  a platform policy change, a team change, or a moment of growth outpacing
  existing systems.
- **Problem/constraint:** limited internal technical capacity, unclear
  digital-property inventory, no documented systems, and/or unclear brand
  architecture across properties.
- **Internal qualification questions** (for future use, not asked today):
  Does the prospective buyer have clear decision-making authority? Is
  there a real, describable digital-property footprint to audit? Is the
  buyer's need bounded (a defined systems/brand/media problem) rather than
  open-ended ("fix everything")? Is there a realistic path to the
  evidence, permission, and contract prerequisites this framework
  requires?
- **Disqualification criteria:** any need that is primarily legal,
  medical, clinical, financial/tax, security-certification, or regulated-
  professional in nature; any expectation of guaranteed revenue, growth,
  or ranking outcomes; any request for representation, endorsement, or
  talent management; any need requiring unbounded ongoing support without
  a separately defined operating model.
- **High-risk/restricted-domain escalation:** any prospective engagement
  touching Health, therapy, tensegrity-as-treatment, legal/political
  representation, or athlete injury/recovery routes immediately to the
  restricted-domain protocol in
  `docs/internal/mark-16-restricted-domain-boundary-addendum.md` — never
  handled as an ordinary Consulting engagement.
- **Readiness signals:** a describable, bounded digital footprint; a named
  decision-maker; willingness to grant scoped access for an Audit;
  willingness to complete the evidence/permission steps in Part E once an
  engagement concludes.
- **Data/privacy flags:** any request involving sensitive personal data,
  credentials, financial records, or legal/medical material triggers
  immediate escalation and is out of scope for this framework — see
  `docs/internal/mark-17-proof-readiness-and-case-study-gates.md` and the
  non-negotiable restriction against storing such material in this
  repository.
- **Decision-maker requirement:** the qualification process (once it
  exists) must confirm the actual person engaging has authority to approve
  scope and evidence release — not assumed, always confirmed.
- **Budget/pricing:** intentionally undecided. No figure, range, or tier
  exists anywhere in this framework.
- **Future intake:** inactive. No form, contact path, or booking mechanism
  is created, modified, or implied by this document.
- **Future evidence/case-study permission requirements:** any future case
  study requires explicit, separately recorded client permission — see
  Part E's `permissions` and `client/public naming status` fields.
